EdTech Readiness Check

A 2-minute diagnostic for classroom teachers, school heads, and community committees before adopting incoming digital tools or donor hardware.

How to Use This Questionnaire

  1. Think about a specific EdTech tool or device your school is considering deploying (e.g., a tablet app, a laptop programme, a donor device).
  2. Read each of the 7 questions carefully. For each question, tick one box that best matches your school's current situation.
  3. Write the point value for your chosen option in the "Score" column at the right of each row.
  4. At the end, add up all 7 scores and write the total in the Summary box on the last section.
  5. Use the Scoring Key below to interpret the total and decide whether to proceed, adapt, or halt deployment.
  6. Completed sheets can be filed as a school decision record or shared with the community committee.
Scoring Key — Total Score Interpretation
🟢 GREEN — Classroom Ready +8 to +20 points Infrastructure and pedagogical conditions broadly support deployment. Proceed with structured classroom integration and ongoing monitoring.
🟡 AMBER — Conditional Readiness 0 to +7 points Significant constraints present. Address critical friction points (power, cost, ratio) before full deployment. Pilot with one class first.
🔴 RED — Not Recommended −22 to −1 points Foundational barriers make deployment high-risk. Redirect to 100% offline-first platforms (e.g., Kolibri, Storybooks Africa, Kiwix) instead.
