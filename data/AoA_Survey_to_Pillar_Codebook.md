# AoA Survey-to-Pillar Codebook
## Analysis A: Construct Validity / Survey-to-Pillar Mapping
**Version:** 1.0 · **Date:** 2026-10-30  
**Instruments:** Teacher Survey (T), Provider Survey (P), Expert Consultation Round 1 (E)  
**Framework:** UNESCO 6 Pillars of Digital Transformation in Education

---

## The 6 Pillars (Reference)

| # | Pillar Label | Core Dimension |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Coordination & Leadership** | National vision, governance, data-informed policy, political continuity |
| 2 | **Connectivity & Infrastructure** | Electricity, internet, devices, hybrid learning spaces |
| 3 | **Cost & Sustainability** | Funding models, OpEx lifecycle, donor dependency, multi-year viability |
| 4 | **Capacity & Culture** | Teacher CPD, digital pedagogy, parental/community engagement |
| 5 | **Content & Solutions** | OER, localised curricula, language fit, contextual software |
| 6 | **Data & Evidence** | Interoperability, data privacy, ethical AI, learning assessment |

## Section 6: Three Core Triangulation Gaps for Analysis B

These are the structural discrepancies the codebook reveals — to be quantified in Analysis B:

### Gap 1: Connectivity Assumption Gap
- **Provider side (P-C2):** What % of provider solutions require broadband vs. fully offline?
- **Teacher side (T-B7):** What % of classrooms have reliable internet?
- **Expert validation (E-F1):** Is infrastructure ranked as top constraint?
- *Hypothesis: provider solutions systematically assume better connectivity than classrooms have.*

### Gap 2: Cost Absorption Gap
- **Provider side (P-E1):** What % of providers depend on donor/grant funding?
- **Provider side (P-C4):** Who pays for mobile data (user vs. covered)?
- **Teacher side (T-D2):** Private tutoring prevalence as cost-willingness signal
- *Gap:* No teacher-side item on personal airtime/device cost expenditure (🔴 survey gap).
- *Hypothesis: teacher personal cost is systematically underestimated by both provider and policy models.*

### Gap 3: Linguistic Inclusion Gap
- **Provider side (P-D1):** What % of products support languages beyond English/French/Portuguese?
- **Teacher side (T-A4, T-E4):** What languages are actually taught in; how AI performs in local languages
- **Expert validation (E-C3):** How close is AI to usable in early-grade local languages?
- *Hypothesis: < 20% of solutions support indigenous SSA languages, while > 50% of classroom instruction uses national/local languages.*
