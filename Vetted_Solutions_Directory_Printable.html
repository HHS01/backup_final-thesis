How to Use This Directory

This reference lists 9 curated educational tools that work in low-connectivity or zero-connectivity environments — the most common condition in Sub-Saharan African and MENA schools. Each entry is a certified Digital Public Good (DPG): open-source or Creative Commons licensed, freely available, with no vendor lock-in.

Use this card to:

  1. Identify which tools suit your school's connectivity level (see "Offline Access" on each entry)
  2. Note the primary URL to download content when you next have internet access
  3. Use the fallback URL if the primary link is unavailable or blocked
  4. Tick the "Tried it?" checkbox after evaluating each tool with students

National Portals (Kenya, Rwanda, Zambia) are curriculum-specific — only relevant to teachers in those countries. All other tools are Pan-African and globally accessible.

Kolibri Learning Platform
Offline DPG Platform 🌍 Pan-African & Global Curator: Learning Equality / UNICEF DPGA

Open-source educational platform engineered for off-grid classrooms. Syncs curriculum units, books, and exercises over local micro-Wi-Fi without consuming cellular airtime. Runs on Raspberry Pi, basic Android tablets, laptops, and any device with a browser — no internet connection ever required once content is loaded.

📶 Offline Access: 100% Offline — Local Wi-Fi / SD Card. Download content once, use forever.
Primary: https://learningequality.org/kolibri/ Fallback: https://github.com/learningequality/kolibri

Kiwix Offline Open Knowledge
Offline DPG Platform 🌍 Pan-African & Global Curator: Kiwix Association / Wikimedia DPG

The gold standard for zero-connectivity schools. Stores complete Wikipedia editions, medical encyclopedias, Wiktionary, and Project Gutenberg books on micro-SD cards without any cellular data. One pre-loaded SD card can give an entire school access to millions of articles, textbooks, and references with no ongoing cost.

📶 Offline Access: 100% Offline — Micro-SD Card / Hotspot ZIM Files. Works with zero data.
Primary: https://www.kiwix.org/ Fallback: https://github.com/kiwix

Language & Literacy 🌍 Pan-African & Global Curator: Ubongo Learning / UNICEF DPGA

National Curriculum Portals — Country-Specific
⚠️ Important: These portals are tied to specific national curricula and broadcasting networks. They are only directly relevant to teachers in Kenya, Rwanda, or Zambia. Zero-rating (free data) only works on local SIM cards within each country.
KICD National Curriculum Broadcasts
National Curriculum 🏛️ National (Kenya) Curator: Kenya Institute of Curriculum Development

Official Competency-Based Curriculum (CBC) lessons broadcast via free-to-air radio and television channels across Kenya, bypassing smartphone and cellular airtime requirements entirely. Content is available in English and Kiswahili, aligned to all grade levels under Kenya's reformed national curriculum.

📶 Offline Access: Broadcast (Radio / TV / Cached MP3). No data cost. Any TV or radio receiver works.
Primary: https://kicd.ac.ke/ Fallback: https://education.go.ke/

Rwanda Basic Education Board (REB) E-Learning
